# Privacy Policy — Tiny Theft Auto

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

Tiny Theft Auto ("the game", "we") is a single-player, fully offline browser/Android
game. This policy explains what data the game handles. **Google Play requires a
publicly hosted privacy policy URL** — publish this file at a stable URL (e.g.
GitHub Pages) and paste that URL into the Play Console listing and the Data Safety
form.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The game has no accounts, no nicknames, no leaderboard, no multiplayer
and no cloud save. It does not send your data to any server.

The game does **not** collect: real names, email, phone, contacts, precise or coarse
location, photos, microphone/camera input, advertising identifiers, health data, or
financial/payment data. There are **no ads** and **no third-party analytics or
tracking SDKs**.

## What stays on your device

Your game progress (in-game money, owned items, settings) is saved only in the
browser's / WebView's local storage on your device, so the game remembers where you
left off. It never leaves the device.

The one piece of online content is optional: the TV inside the ranch house, when you
turn it on, loads a web page (`andredarcie.github.io/andre-os`, served by GitHub
Pages). The rest of the game never touches the network.

## Children

The game depicts cartoon/action violence and is **not directed at children**. Set
the target audience accordingly in the Play Console (Teen/Mature, not "Designed for
Families").

## Your choices / deletion

You can erase your saved progress any time by clearing the site data in your browser,
or on Android via **Settings → Apps → Tiny Theft Auto → Storage → Clear data**.
